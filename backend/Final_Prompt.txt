KEEP = semantic evolution
The NEW message gives the reader meaningfully better understanding of the change (what exactly was done, why, technical details, impact, design decisions, affected components, etc.).

REMOVE = non-semantic
Only grammar, formatting, tags, issue numbers, pure shortening, or cosmetic rewording without adding real insight.

CRITICAL DECISION RULE:
If the NEW description helps an engineer better understand WHAT changed, WHY it was done, or HOW it works — even if subtle — it is KEEP.
Do NOT dismiss refactoring summaries, added context, clarifications, or technical details as "just wording".
